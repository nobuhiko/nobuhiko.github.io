Kasane: Deleting Your Data

Kasane (developer: nobuhiko) does not create accounts and does not store your photos or records on the developer's server. All data is stored only on your device, so you can delete it completely on your own device.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open the Settings tab
  2. Under "Data", tap "Delete everything (including photos)"

This deletes all photos, notes, temperature records and settings. To delete a single day, open that day's page and tap "Remove this page".

Delete the app

Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on your device.

What is deleted, and what remains

  • Deleted: photos (reduced-size copies and thumbnails), notes, temperature and weather, locations, settings, and cached temperatures
  • Kept by the developer: nothing (nothing is collected)
  • The rounded location and date used to get the temperature are used only to process that request and are not stored

Questions and deletion requests
